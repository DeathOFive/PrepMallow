# PrepMallow

PrepMallow is a free toolkit for getting photos, PDFs, and other files ready for online forms, email, and sharing. The Android app runs all 17 tools offline and processes files on your device.

**[Download PrepMallow v1.0.1 for Android](https://github.com/DeathOFive/PrepMallow/releases/download/v1.0.1/PrepMallow-v1.0.1.apk)** · [Release notes](https://github.com/DeathOFive/PrepMallow/releases/tag/v1.0.1) · [Use PrepMallow on the web](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/)

## Install on Android

Requires **Android 10 or newer** and **Android System WebView 110 or newer**.

1. On your Android device, download `PrepMallow-v1.0.1.apk` from the **Assets** section of the [v1.0.1 release](https://github.com/DeathOFive/PrepMallow/releases/tag/v1.0.1).
2. Open the APK from **Downloads**. If prompted, tap **Settings** and turn on **Allow from this source** for the browser or Files app that opened the APK.
3. Return to the installer, tap **Install**, and open **PrepMallow** from your app launcher.
4. Pick a tool and select the files you want to prepare. Tap **Save** or **Share** when your result is ready.

If you have the v1.0.0 preview installed, v1.0.1 appears alongside it as a separate app.

## What you can do

| Need | Tools |
| --- | --- |
| Fit a photo into an upload limit | [Compress images](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/compress-image/) to a target size such as 100 KB or 200 KB, [resize](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/resize-image/) to exact pixel dimensions, or [convert images](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/convert-image/) in formats such as HEIC, JPG, PNG, and WebP. |
| Prepare a PDF | [Combine photos into one PDF](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/images-to-pdf/), [merge PDFs](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/merge-pdf/), [reorder, rotate, or extract pages](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/organize-pdf/), [export PDF pages as images](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/pdf-to-images/), or [compress a scanned PDF](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/compress-pdf/). |
| Get files ready to share | [Add a text watermark](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/watermark-file/), [remove photo metadata](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/remove-image-metadata/), [rename files in batches](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/rename-files/), or [create a ZIP](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/create-zip/). |
| Check a form’s requirements | [Check a file](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/tools/check-file/) against its required size, format, or image dimensions. |

For a form that asks for a JPG under 200 KB and exactly 600 × 600 pixels, open **Compress images**, set the size and dimensions, and save the result. Then use **Check file details** to verify the copy before uploading it.

## File privacy

The web version processes selected files in your browser. The Android app handles them on your device, even when you’re offline. [Read about file handling](https://saaimsohail.com/prepmallow/privacy/).
