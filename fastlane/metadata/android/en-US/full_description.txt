Get your files ready to send. PrepMallow offers 17 tools for photos, PDFs and ZIPs, with processing on your device and no account required.

Images
• Compress photos to a target file size.
• Resize to exact dimensions or fit inside a box.
• Convert HEIC, JPG, PNG and WebP images.
• Crop, rotate and flip photos.
• Remove image metadata from a new copy.

PDFs
• Combine photos into a PDF.
• Merge documents and select page ranges.
• Reorder, rotate, extract and split pages.
• Export PDF pages as images.
• Compress scanned or photo-heavy PDFs.
• Extract selectable text.

File utilities
• Add text watermarks to photos or PDF pages.
• Rename files in batches.
• Create ZIP archives, or browse and extract their contents.
• Check file size, format and image dimensions against a form's requirements.

Use Android's file and folder pickers to choose documents, send files to PrepMallow from another app, and save or share the results through Android's system dialogs.

All tools are bundled in the app and work offline. PrepMallow does not request the Internet permission and includes no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Requires Android 10 or newer and Android System WebView 110 or newer. Encrypted PDFs, password-protected ZIPs, ZIP64 archives and OCR of image-only scans are not supported. Compression results depend on the input.
